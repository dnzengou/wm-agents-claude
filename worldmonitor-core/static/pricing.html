Billing FAQ

Do I need a credit card for the Free tier?

No. The Free tier works without a card. You only enter billing details if you choose to upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from the Stripe billing portal at any time. You keep paid-tier features until the end of the current billing period.

What happens when I hit the Free tier's alert cap?

The Add button in the alerts panel is disabled with an inline upgrade prompt. Existing alerts keep firing — you just can't add a 4th until you upgrade to Pro.

Is there a Team / annual discount?

Not yet — annual billing is on the roadmap. If you need a Team plan with SSO or an invoice-based agreement, see the About page for the maintainer contact.
